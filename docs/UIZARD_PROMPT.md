# Prompt Uizard — Desain Aplikasi "Semesta" (5 Okt 2026)

> Tempel seluruh teks di bawah ke Uizard Autodesigner apa adanya.
> Bahasa prompt: Inggris (hasil generate lebih akurat), isi UI: Bahasa Indonesia.

---

Design a mobile app called "Semesta" — a cheerful family learning app for physics
and mathematics. Target users: kids (8+), teens, parents, and young adults learning
together as a family. Style: flat vector, cheerful Indonesian children's storybook
illustration, rounded corners (16–24px), generous spacing, clean and friendly,
mobile-first for Android (360px wide), thumb-friendly with big touch targets.

IMPORTANT: All visible UI text must be in Bahasa Indonesia (not English).

Color palette (Baby Blue theme):
- Primary Baby Blue #89CFF0 — highlights, active states, illustration accents
- Deep Blue #1E6FB8 — buttons, headers, CTAs (for contrast on white)
- Warm Amber #FFC53D — badges, streak flame, small accents
- Background #F4FAFE (very light blue tint), Surface #FFFFFF, Text #1E293B
- Success #22C55E, Warning #F59E0B

Typography: "Plus Jakarta Sans", rounded and friendly. Headings bold, body regular.

Generate these screens:

1. Splash: app logo (simple atom with two orbits, baby blue + amber on deep blue),
   app name "Semesta", tagline "Dari nol sampai ahli, dalam satu tempat",
   baby blue gradient background, "Mulai Belajar" button.

2. Profile picker: title "Siapa yang belajar?" — avatar cards for Kirana (kid),
   Dimas (teen), Bu Ratna (parent), Arya (young adult), plus a dashed
   "Tambah profil" card.

3. Home / Katalog: greeting "Halo, Kirana!" with streak flame "🔥 5 hari";
   search bar "Cari konsep..."; a "Lanjutkan belajar" card (Gerak Parabola · L2,
   60% progress bar, play button); filter chips (Semua, Fisika, Matematika,
   L0, L1, L2, L3); grid of concept cards each with icon, title, progress ring,
   and tiny badges for content types (cerita, simulasi, studi kasus).

4. Concept detail "Gerak Parabola": level selector pills L0–L5; lesson block list
   with icons — Cerita bergambar, Simulasi interaktif, Studi kasus industri,
   Latihan & Kuis; "Mulai pelajaran" CTA button.

5. Story viewer: full-width flat-vector illustration panel, caption and dialogue
   bubble, dots indicator (panel 3 of 6), "Lanjut" button, subtle
   "Lewati ke simulasi" link.

6. Simulation: canvas area showing a projectile trajectory arcing toward a target;
   sliders "Sudut 45°" and "Kecepatan 20 m/s"; HUD chip "Jangkauan: 40,8 m";
   buttons Mulai / Jeda / Reset; expandable "Lihat rumus" section.

7. Quiz: "Soal 2 dari 5" progress, question card, 4 answer option cards,
   "Petunjuk" button, "Periksa" CTA.

8. Quiz result: trophy flat illustration, "Hebat! Skor 80", "+24 XP" chip,
   animated mastery progress bar, recommendation button
   "Lanjut ke: Momentum & Tumbukan".

9. Peta Keterampilan: zoomable node graph of concepts linked by prerequisite
   lines; nodes colored by status (gray = tersedia, baby blue = sedang dipelajari,
   green = dikuasai, amber = perlu diulang); bottom sheet showing selected
   concept info.

10. Progress dashboard: streak flame "🔥 12 hari", "⭐ 1.240 XP", level badge;
    "Penguasaan konsep" horizontal bars; amber "Perlu diulang: Persen" warning
    card; badge shelf row.

11. Profile & settings: profile header, UI mode selector
    (Penjelajah / Pelajar / Ahli), theme toggle, "Unduhan offline" row with
    storage sizes, "Ekspor data" row.

Bottom navigation bar on main screens with 4 tabs: Katalog, Peta, Progres, Profil.
